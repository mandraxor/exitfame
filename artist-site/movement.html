EXIT FAME / 02 // THE MOVEMENT

The Movement

"Exit is not a stage name — it's a declaration." The real journey of Eric Henderson from Boston streets to recovery leadership.

2019
THE DECLARATION

Shedding "Fame Flynt" & Choosing Sobriety

Born Eric Henderson in Boston, raised with deep roots in Roxbury and Dorchester. Known formerly as "Fame Flynt", the pivotal turning point came in 2019 following the loss of his father. Eric made a life-defining choice: exit street lifestyle, drop hollow recognition, and enter active sobriety. He declared himself Exit Fame—turning survival into sound.

2021
THE FRONTLINES

Rikers Island & Certified Recovery Coaching

Direct outreach in rehabilitation facilities, sober living programs, and correctional institutions. Exit took his authentic hip-hop testimony directly into Rikers Island, proving that vulnerable, conscious storytelling breaks cycles of recidivism and addiction.

2023
YOUTH IMPACT

High School & College Assemblies

Commanding rooms of 800 to 1,000 students across New England high schools and college auditoriums. Exit bridges the raw energy of urban music with actionable recovery and mental health empowerment.

2024
CRITICAL ACCLAIM

Boston Music Awards Triumph

Recognized as New Hip-Hop Artist of the Year at the Boston Music Awards and Mega Awards. "Last Shall Be First" solidifies Exit as a dominant, conscious force in East Coast rap.

2026
GLOBAL EXPANSION

6+ Years Active Sobriety & Onyx World Tour

Reaching over 6 consecutive years of verified sobriety. Released Permanent Stains (debuting at #7 on Apple iTunes Hip-Hop) and joined hip-hop legends Onyx, Busta Rhymes, and KRS-One across 11 countries on the "Shut 'Em Down" International Tour.
