01

The Artist

Exit is not a stage name — it's a declaration. A Boston-bred hip hop artist who turned survival into a sound, Exit's music carries the weight of real experience and the momentum of someone who refused to stay stuck. Commanding crowds of 800 to 1,000 fans per show, Exit has built a loyal following that doesn't just listen — they move.


With the 2024 Boston Music Award for New Hip Hop Artist of the Year now in hand, Exit steps into the next chapter armed with industry recognition, community trust, and a vision that reaches far beyond the stage.

"Exit doesn't just make music — he makes movements. Every verse is a blueprint for getting out."

— Boston Music Awards, 2024

WHY
IT MATTERS
Exit's reach extends far beyond venues and playlists. His story resonates powerfully in recovery centers, group homes, Rikers Island, and schools — spaces where young people need proof that another path exists. The message of prevention through art is not just compelling; it's massive in scope, and Exit is positioned to be that voice at scale. Colleges, youth programs, and institutional partners represent an enormous and largely untapped opportunity — one Exit is ready to take.
